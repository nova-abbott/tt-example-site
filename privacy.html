Last updated: 2026-10-30

Nova Assistant is a personal AI assistant operated for Dr. Chester Cheng (鄭中). It connects to Google Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Calendar using OAuth, solely to carry out tasks requested by its owner.

What we access

  • Gmail: sending messages and reading messages the owner asks about.
  • Google Drive: reading and uploading files the owner asks about.
  • Google Calendar: listing and creating events the owner asks about.

How data is used

Data is used only to complete the owner's requests. It is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and it is not used for advertising or to train models.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored on the operator's own machine with restricted file permissions. They are not published or committed to any repository.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time in your Google Account under Security → Third-party access (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Revoking access stops all further use of your data.
